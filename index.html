About this survey

The African Circuit is mapping where private capital is needed across African markets, where investors want to deploy it, and what stops the two from meeting.

What your answers mean for us:

  • They are combined with other responses into the African Capital & Investment findings report. Individual answers are never published or attributed to you.
  • They shape the Circuit's programme, panels and matchmaking across its markets.
  • If you choose to opt in at the end, we may contact you privately where we identify relevant alignment.

It is for businesses and projects seeking capital, investors looking to deploy it, and the advisers and institutions working between them.

Your information is processed in line with the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 by The Philanthropic Foundation (Reg. No. 679), operator of The African Circuit, used only for this research and related follow-up, never sold, and you may ask to access, correct or delete it at any time (Privacy Policy).

Participation does not constitute an offer, solicitation, financing commitment or investment recommendation. Information supplied is treated confidentially and remains subject to independent assessment and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
